How a generation is made

Dozens of versions of a model are scored on real outcomes. The strongest are selected as parents. Their settings are combined to make children, small random changes are introduced, and the children are scored again. Settings that work together survive together. In genetics, that interaction is called epistasis, and it is why we search as a population instead of tuning one dial at a time.

What never gets traded away

  • Floors on the findings that kill fast or cost most
  • Performance on the worst site, not the average
  • The gap between groups the model serves
  • Reasons a regulator can read

The output is a set of models with different tradeoffs. Your team chooses the operating point. The model never decides for you.
